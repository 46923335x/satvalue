Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026

Minimal analytics

SatValue records aggregate page-view counts to understand which pages are used. These analytics events do not intentionally include names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, or precise location.

Server logs

Like most web services, SatValue's hosting and server infrastructure may process and retain limited request information—including IP address, request path, timestamp, browser information, and error details—for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

Market-data requests

Ticker searches are sent to the SatValue server to retrieve market data. Do not enter personal or confidential information in the ticker field.

Third-party services

Pages may load chart libraries from third-party content-delivery networks and include links to market-data providers and fund issuers. Those providers receive ordinary web-request information and govern it under their own privacy policies.
